# Structured output | session-discipline

**Load when:** a response carries more than roughly three findings, options or items the contributor has to act on — an audit result, a triage, a set of open questions, a comparison of approaches.

---

## Shape the response for the decision

Length is governed by token efficiency; this governs shape. Structure the response so the decision is visible without reading all of it.

- **Lead with the verdict or the ask.** One line, before any supporting detail. A reader who stops after the first line knows the outcome and what is wanted from them.
- **Tabulate anything enumerable or comparative.** Findings, options, costs, trade-offs. Prose carries a single argument; a table carries a set.
- **Mark what needs attention.** Where items differ in weight, say which few matter and why.
- **Put the ask in one place, at the end, under its own heading.**
- **Caveats follow the ask.**
- **Cut supporting prose to what changes the decision.** Other detail belongs in the file, the plan or the issues log.

A long response is acceptable where the content warrants it. An unstructured one is not.

**The test:** a contributor who has to read the response twice to find what is asked of them was given the wrong shape, whatever its length.
